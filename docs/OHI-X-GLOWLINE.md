# OHI-X Glowline™

**Canonical ACC route:** `/ohi-x/glowline`

OHI-X Glowline™ is registered in ACC as a ONEGODIAN, LLC product-development program for intelligent illumination, guidance and environmental wayfinding.

## Product family

- Glowline Path™ — V1 priority
- Glowline Home™
- Glowline Event™
- Glowline Building™
- Glowline Safety™
- Glowline Transit™ / Glowline Road™
- Glowline Digital™
- Glowline Onegodia™

## Architecture

`Physical Layer → Control Layer → Intelligence Layer → Management Layer`

The Physical Layer may use established illumination, photoluminescent, reflective, solar, controller and sensor technologies. The Control Layer governs zones, timing, brightness and patterns. The Intelligence Layer is reserved for implemented and validated adaptive OHI-X functionality. The Management Layer surfaces configuration, installations, evidence, diagnostics and lifecycle state through ACC.

## V1

Glowline Path™ is the first commercial prototype target, initially focused on controlled private-property environments rather than regulated public-road infrastructure.

## Governance rule

ACC may coordinate product records, tasks, workflows, approvals, deployment records, verification evidence and operational status. Planned intelligence, quantum behavior, autonomous behavior, safety performance and other unvalidated capabilities must not be represented as production facts. Human approval remains required for final pricing, regulated deployments, safety acceptance and legally operative decisions.
